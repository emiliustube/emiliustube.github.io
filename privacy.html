Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy covers iOS apps published by Emil Itskovich (including SHIFTROOM and Suds Lane).

What we collect

These games do not require an account and do not ask you for your name, email, or payment card for gameplay.

If you allow App Tracking Transparency (ATT), advertising partners may use the device’s advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, ads may still appear in a limited / non-personalized form.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads that support free gameplay. Google may collect device and usage data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance.

Local game progress

Progress and settings are stored on your device (for example via on-device preferences). We do not operate a cloud save account for these titles.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13 and are not in Apple’s Kids Category.

Contact

Questions: use the App Store support / marketing site emiliustube.github.io or the developer contact listed on each app’s product page.
